# What is an eSIM

## Overview
An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM that allows customers to activate a mobile data plan without a physical SIM card. TravelWifi eSIM plans provide mobile data for travelers — the customer receives a QR code after purchase, scans it with their phone, and gets instant connectivity in their destination country or region.

**Key facts for support:**
- No physical device is shipped — this is a digital product (QR code delivery)
- The customer must have an **eSIM-compatible phone** (most phones from 2020 onwards)
- One phone can hold multiple eSIMs (e.g., one for Japan, one for Europe)
- eSIM plans are **data-only** — no voice calls or SMS (customers use WhatsApp, FaceTime, etc.)
- Plans use the **TOTAL_DATA** model: a fixed pool of data, valid for a set number of days
- The eSIM is embedded in the phone's hardware — there is no removable chip

## What the customer receives
After purchasing, the customer receives:
1. A **QR code** (via email or in their account)
2. Instructions for installing the eSIM on their device
3. An ICCID number (the unique SIM identifier — starts with `89`)

The eSIM can be installed **before** traveling but will only activate and start counting days once the customer arrives at the destination and connects to a local network.

## eSIM vs physical SIM
| Feature | eSIM | Physical SIM |
|---|---|---|
| Form factor | Built into phone chip | Removable card |
| Installation | Scan QR code | Insert into SIM tray |
| Multiple plans | Yes — store 5-10 profiles | One SIM per slot |
| Switch plans | Toggle in Settings | Physically swap cards |
| Lost/damaged | Cannot be lost | Can be lost or broken |
| Delivery | Instant (digital) | Shipping required |

# How to install an eSIM — iPhone

## Before you start
- Make sure you have a WiFi or mobile data connection (needed to download the eSIM profile)
- Have the QR code ready (from the email or your TravelWifi account — display it on another screen or print it)
- Make sure your iPhone is running iOS 12.1 or later (Settings → General → About → Software Version)

## Installation steps (iOS 17 / iOS 18)
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Cellular** (or **Mobile Data** in some regions)
3. Tap **Add eSIM** (or **Add Cellular Plan**)
4. Tap **Use QR Code**
5. Point your iPhone camera at the QR code
6. When the carrier plan appears, tap **Continue**
7. Wait for the plan to activate (may take 1-2 minutes)
8. When asked to label the plan, choose a descriptive name like **"Travel Data"** or **"TravelWifi Japan"**
9. When asked **"Which line do you want to use for cellular data?"** — select the **TravelWifi eSIM**
10. Tap **Done**

## Installation steps (iOS 14 / 15 / 16)
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Cellular** (or **Mobile Data**)
3. Tap **Add Cellular Plan**
4. Scan the QR code with the camera
5. Tap **Add Cellular Plan** when prompted
6. Label the plan and tap **Continue**
7. Choose data line settings and tap **Done**

## After installation
- Go to **Settings → Cellular** — you should see the new plan listed
- Make sure it says **Turn On This Line** is enabled
- **Critical:** Enable **Data Roaming** for the eSIM line:
  - Settings → Cellular → tap the TravelWifi plan → **Data Roaming → ON**
- Set the TravelWifi eSIM as your **Cellular Data** line:
  - Settings → Cellular → **Cellular Data** → select TravelWifi plan

## iPhone-specific tips
- You can install the eSIM days before travel — the validity countdown doesn't start until you connect at the destination
- US-model iPhone 14/15/16 have **no physical SIM tray** — they use eSIM only
- If you already have a primary carrier eSIM, the TravelWifi eSIM will be your second line
- You can keep your primary line active for calls/texts while using TravelWifi for data

# How to install an eSIM — Samsung

## Before you start
- Make sure you have a WiFi or mobile data connection
- Have the QR code ready on another screen or printed
- Samsung phone must be running One UI 3.0 or later
- Confirm your Samsung model supports eSIM (see compatibility section)

## Installation steps (One UI 5 / 6 — Samsung Galaxy S23, S24, Z Flip/Fold)
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Connections**
3. Tap **SIM manager** (or **SIM card manager**)
4. Tap **Add eSIM**
5. Tap **Scan QR code from service provider**
6. Point your camera at the QR code
7. Wait for the network to verify (1-2 minutes)
8. When prompted, tap **Confirm**
9. The eSIM will download and install
10. Toggle the eSIM **ON** in SIM manager

## Installation steps (One UI 4 — Samsung Galaxy S22, S21)
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Connections**
3. Tap **SIM card manager**
4. Tap **Add mobile plan**
5. Tap **Scan carrier QR code**
6. Scan the QR code
7. Tap **Confirm** and wait for activation

## After installation
- Go to **Settings → Connections → SIM manager**
- Make sure the TravelWifi eSIM is toggled ON
- Set **Mobile data** to use the TravelWifi eSIM
- **Critical:** Enable **Data Roaming**:
  - Settings → Connections → Mobile networks → **Data roaming → ON**
- If you have a physical SIM installed, make sure the TravelWifi eSIM is set as the data SIM

## Samsung-specific tips
- Some Samsung phones may show "Add mobile plan" instead of "Add eSIM"
- If the QR scan doesn't work, try entering the activation code manually (the long string starting with `LPA:1$`)
- Samsung Dual SIM phones can use physical SIM for calls + eSIM for data simultaneously
- Restart the phone if the eSIM doesn't appear in SIM manager after installation

# How to install an eSIM — Google Pixel and other Android

## Google Pixel installation
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Network & internet**
3. Tap **SIMs** (or **Mobile network**)
4. Tap **+ (Add)** or **Download a SIM instead**
5. Tap **Next** when prompted
6. Tap **Scan QR code** (or point camera at QR code)
7. Wait for the carrier plan to load
8. Tap **Confirm** and wait for download
9. Toggle the new eSIM **ON**
10. Set it as the data SIM when prompted

## Generic Android installation (Motorola, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Oppo)
The exact menu path varies by manufacturer, but generally:
1. Open **Settings**
2. Look for **Network & internet**, **Connections**, or **SIM cards & mobile networks**
3. Tap **Add eSIM**, **Add mobile plan**, or **Download SIM**
4. Select **Scan QR code**
5. Point the camera at the QR code
6. Confirm the installation
7. Enable the eSIM and set it as the data line

## After installation (all Android)
- Verify the eSIM appears in network settings
- Set TravelWifi eSIM as the **mobile data** line
- **Critical:** Enable **Data Roaming**:
  - Settings → Network & internet → [TravelWifi SIM] → **Roaming → ON**
- Restart the phone if the eSIM doesn't connect within 5 minutes

## Manual activation code entry
If the QR code scanner doesn't work (camera issue, screen glare, etc.), the customer can enter the activation code manually:
1. In the "Add eSIM" screen, look for **"Can't scan?"** or **"Enter code manually"**
2. Enter the activation code — this is a long string starting with `LPA:1$` followed by the server address and activation code
3. This code should be in the same email as the QR code
4. Tap **Confirm** to proceed

# How to remove or delete an eSIM

## When to remove an eSIM
- The plan is fully used/expired and the customer no longer needs it
- The customer wants to free up eSIM slots for new plans
- Troubleshooting: removing and reinstalling can fix some activation issues

**Important warning:** Removing an eSIM **permanently deletes** the profile from the phone. If the plan still has active data, the customer will need a **new QR code** to reinstall. They cannot reuse the original QR code.

## Remove eSIM on iPhone
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Cellular** (or **Mobile Data**)
3. Tap the TravelWifi eSIM plan you want to remove
4. Scroll down and tap **Delete eSIM** (or **Remove Cellular Plan**)
5. Confirm by tapping **Delete** in the pop-up

## Remove eSIM on Samsung
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Connections**
3. Tap **SIM manager**
4. Tap the TravelWifi eSIM
5. Tap **Remove** (or toggle OFF then tap Remove)
6. Confirm removal

## Remove eSIM on Google Pixel / Android
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Network & internet**
3. Tap **SIMs**
4. Tap the TravelWifi eSIM
5. Tap **Delete SIM** or **Erase SIM**
6. Confirm

## Support notes on removal
- Removing an eSIM does NOT cancel the plan or trigger a refund — the plan continues to exist on TelliSIM's side
- If a customer accidentally removes an active eSIM, they will need a new QR code to reinstall
- Inactive/expired eSIMs can be safely removed — they serve no purpose and take up eSIM slots
- Most phones store up to 5-10 eSIM profiles; customers rarely need to remove unless they travel frequently

# How to switch between eSIMs

## Using multiple eSIMs on the same phone
Modern phones can store multiple eSIM profiles (5-10 depending on the model). However, only **one or two** can be active at a time. Customers traveling to multiple countries may have separate eSIMs for each destination.

## Switch data line on iPhone
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Cellular**
3. Tap **Cellular Data**
4. Select the eSIM plan you want to use for internet
5. The switch is instant — no restart needed

Alternatively, toggle individual plans on/off:
1. Settings → Cellular → tap a plan → **Turn On This Line** (toggle ON or OFF)

## Switch data line on Samsung
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Connections**
3. Tap **SIM manager**
4. Under **Mobile data**, select the eSIM you want for internet
5. Toggle eSIM plans ON/OFF as needed

## Switch data line on Google Pixel / Android
1. Open **Settings**
2. Tap **Network & internet**
3. Tap **SIMs**
4. Tap the eSIM you want to activate and toggle it ON
5. Set it as the preferred data SIM

## Best practices
- Keep the primary carrier SIM active for calls/texts, use TravelWifi eSIM for data only
- Disable the TravelWifi eSIM when not in the covered country to avoid unnecessary network scanning
- When moving between countries with different local eSIMs, disable the old country's eSIM and enable the new one
- Label eSIMs clearly (e.g., "Japan Travel", "Europe Trip") to avoid confusion

# eSIM plan types

## Local plans (single country)
A local plan provides data in **one specific country**. This is the most popular option for travelers visiting a single destination.

**SKU format:** `{COUNTRY}_{GB}GB_{DAYS}D`
**Examples:**
- `US_10GB_30D` → United States, 10 GB, valid 30 days
- `JP_5GB_15D` → Japan, 5 GB, valid 15 days
- `FR_20GB_30D` → France, 20 GB, valid 30 days
- `TH_1GB_7D` → Thailand, 1 GB, valid 7 days

**Unlimited local plans** are also available for select countries:
- Format: `{COUNTRY}_{DAYS}D_Unlimited`
- Example: `ES_7D_Unlimited` → Spain, unlimited data, 7 days

**Available data sizes:** 1 GB, 3 GB, 5 GB, 10 GB, 20 GB (varies by country)
**Validity periods:** 7 days, 15 days, 30 days (varies by country)

## Regional plans (multi-country)
A regional plan covers **multiple countries within a region** with a single eSIM. Ideal for customers traveling across borders (e.g., a Europe trip visiting France, Italy, and Spain).

**SKU format:** `{Region}_{TIER}_{GB}GB_{DAYS}D`
**Example:** `Europe_STANDARD_10GB_30D` → Europe Standard tier, 10 GB, 30 days

Regional plans come in **three tiers** based on coverage breadth:

| Tier | Coverage | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **LITE** | ~25% of countries in the region (cheapest destinations) | Budget travelers, single-hub trips |
| **STANDARD** | ~60% of countries in the region | Most common, covers major destinations |
| **PLUS** | 100% of countries in the region | Full coverage, multi-country trips |

**Available regions:** Europe, Asia, Latin America (LATAM), Africa, Middle East, North America, Caribbean, Oceania

## Global plans (worldwide)
A global plan covers countries **across multiple regions** with a single eSIM. Best for round-the-world travelers or multi-continent business trips.

**SKU format:** `GLOBAL_{TIER}_{GB}GB_{DAYS}D`
**Example:** `GLOBAL_PLUS_20GB_30D` → Global Plus tier, 20 GB, 30 days

Global plans also use LITE / STANDARD / PLUS tiers with the same coverage logic.

# How the data model works

## TOTAL_DATA model
All eSIM plans use the **TOTAL_DATA** model. This means:

1. The customer buys a **fixed pool of data** (e.g., 10 GB) valid for a **set number of days** (e.g., 30 days)
2. They can use data at any pace — burn it all on day 1 if they want
3. The plan ends when **either** condition is met:
   - The data pool is exhausted (all GB used up), **OR**
   - The validity period expires (e.g., 30 days have passed)
4. **Whichever comes first** ends the plan

## No throttling — plan dies when data is gone
Unlike rental plans, eSIM plans have **no Fair Usage Policy (FUP)** and **no speed throttling**. When the data runs out, the connection simply stops. The customer must purchase a top-up to continue.

## When does the validity period start?
- The clock starts when the eSIM **first connects to a mobile network** at the destination
- Installing the eSIM does NOT start the countdown
- Sitting in an airport with WiFi does NOT start the countdown
- The moment the phone registers on a partner cellular network, the plan is "activated" and the days begin counting

## Support talking points
- **"My internet stopped working on day 3"** — Check CDR consumption. If the customer burned through all their data in 3 days, the plan is exhausted. This is normal behavior, not a defect. Offer a top-up.
- **"I still have days left but no data"** — The data pool was consumed before the validity expired. Normal. Offer a top-up.
- **"My plan expired but I still have data"** — The validity period ended before all data was used. Normal — unused data does not carry over.
- **"How much data do I have left?"** — Check TelliSIM lifecycle: `remaining_gb` field.

## Data usage estimates
| Activity | Approximate data usage |
|---|---|
| Email (text only) | ~0.05 MB per email |
| Web browsing | ~2-5 MB per page |
| Social media (scrolling) | ~50-100 MB per hour |
| Google Maps / navigation | ~5-10 MB per hour |
| Music streaming | ~70-150 MB per hour |
| Video call (WhatsApp/FaceTime) | ~300-500 MB per hour |
| Video streaming (SD) | ~700 MB per hour |
| Video streaming (HD) | ~2-3 GB per hour |
| Cloud backup / photo sync | Unlimited — can drain entire plan |

## Data-saving tips for customers
- Disable automatic cloud backups (iCloud Photos, Google Photos) while on eSIM data
- Download maps offline (Google Maps → download area) before going out
- Download music/podcasts/videos on WiFi before leaving hotel
- Set video streaming apps to "low data" or "WiFi only" mode
- Close background apps that consume data (social media auto-play, app updates)
- Use WiFi at hotels, cafes, and restaurants for large downloads
- Disable auto-play videos on social media apps

# Top-ups and refills

## How top-ups work
If a customer runs out of data before their validity expires (or wants more data for an extended stay), they can purchase a **top-up** — an additional data package loaded onto the same eSIM.

**Key facts:**
- The top-up is applied to the **same ICCID** (same eSIM) — no new QR code needed
- The customer does not need to remove or reinstall the eSIM
- Top-up data is **added** to any remaining balance
- Top-up validity is independent — the new package has its own expiration window
- Multiple top-ups can be stacked on the same eSIM

## How to identify a top-up in the system
- The same ICCID appears on **multiple non-cancelled orders**
- The later order is flagged as `is_topup: true` in the allocation ledger
- Example: Order A-1001 sells `FR_5GB_30D` to ICCID 8901...AAA, then Order A-1440 sells `FR_10GB_30D` to the same ICCID → A-1440 is a top-up

## Common top-up issues
- **"I topped up but nothing happened"** — Check TelliSIM lifecycle: does a newer plan attachment exist? If the order completed but TelliSIM doesn't show it, the top-up may not have been provisioned. Escalate to carrier ops.
- **"Can I top up with a different country?"** — Yes, if TelliSIM supports it. The eSIM can hold plans for different destinations.
- **"Can I top up before my current plan expires?"** — Yes. The new data is added alongside the existing plan.

# Coverage — Global plans

## What global plans cover
Global plans provide coverage across **multiple continents** with a single eSIM. They are ideal for:
- Multi-destination trips (e.g., Europe + Asia in one trip)
- Business travelers visiting multiple regions
- Customers who want one plan without worrying about country-specific purchases

## Global tier coverage
- **GLOBAL LITE** — Covers ~40 of the most popular travel destinations worldwide
- **GLOBAL STANDARD** — Covers ~80 countries across all major regions
- **GLOBAL PLUS** — Maximum coverage, 100+ countries across all regions

## When to recommend Global vs Regional
| Scenario | Recommend |
|---|---|
| Visiting 1 country | **Local plan** — cheapest option |
| Visiting 2-3 countries in same region | **Regional plan** — good value for multi-country |
| Visiting countries across 2+ regions | **Global plan** — one plan, no hassle |
| Round-the-world trip | **Global PLUS** — maximum coverage |
| Budget-conscious, popular destinations only | **Global LITE** — fewer countries but lower cost |

# Troubleshooting — eSIM won't install

## QR code scan fails
1. **Screen brightness** — Make sure the screen displaying the QR code is at full brightness
2. **Distance and angle** — Hold the phone 6-8 inches from the QR code, straight on (not angled)
3. **Camera focus** — Tap the QR code on screen to focus the camera. Clean the camera lens if blurry
4. **QR code quality** — If viewing on a screen, zoom in. If printed, make sure it's not smudged or distorted
5. **Manual entry fallback** — Use the activation code (LPA:1$...) instead. Look for "Enter details manually" or "Can't scan?"

## "eSIM cannot be added" or "This eSIM is no longer available"
- The QR code has **already been scanned** — each code is one-time use. The customer needs a replacement code
- The eSIM profile was **already installed** on another device — it cannot be moved; contact support for a new code
- The phone is **carrier-locked** and blocking third-party eSIM installation

## "No connection" or "Unable to activate"
- Check that the phone has an **active internet connection** (WiFi or mobile data) — this is required to download the profile
- Try a different WiFi network if the current one is blocking eSIM activation (some hotel/airport WiFi blocks it)
- **Restart the phone** and try again
- Check if the phone's **date and time** are set correctly (Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically)

## "Maximum number of eSIMs reached"
- The phone has hit its eSIM profile limit (typically 5-10 depending on model)
- The customer needs to **remove an unused/expired eSIM** to free up a slot (see "How to remove an eSIM")
- On iPhone: Settings → Cellular → tap expired plan → Delete eSIM

## Phone not listed as compatible but customer insists
- Ask for the exact model name and number (Settings → About Phone)
- Some regional variants of the same phone may or may not support eSIM
- Carrier firmware can disable eSIM on otherwise-compatible hardware
- Suggest the customer contact their carrier to confirm eSIM support

# Troubleshooting — no data connection

## Step-by-step diagnosis
Work through this checklist in order. The #1 and #2 causes account for ~80% of "no data" tickets.

### Step 1: Data Roaming (THE MOST COMMON ISSUE)
- **iPhone:** Settings → Cellular → [TravelWifi plan] → **Data Roaming must be ON**
- **Samsung:** Settings → Connections → Mobile networks → **Data roaming must be ON**
- **Pixel:** Settings → Network & internet → [TravelWifi SIM] → **Roaming must be ON**
- Without data roaming enabled, the eSIM **cannot connect** to partner networks abroad

### Step 2: Correct data line selected
The phone may be routing data through the primary SIM instead of the TravelWifi eSIM:
- **iPhone:** Settings → Cellular → **Cellular Data** → select TravelWifi plan
- **Samsung:** Settings → Connections → **SIM manager** → Mobile data → select TravelWifi
- **Pixel:** Settings → Network & internet → SIMs → set TravelWifi as data SIM

### Step 3: eSIM plan status
Check TelliSIM lifecycle state:
- `PENDING` / `ASSIGNED` → Plan not yet activated. Customer needs to be at the destination with data roaming ON
- `ACTIVE` / `ENABLED` → Plan is live. Move to next step
- `EXPIRED` → Plan validity ended. Offer top-up or new plan
- `DISABLED` → Plan terminated. Escalate to carrier ops

### Step 4: Data remaining
Check `remaining_gb` from TelliSIM:
- If < 0.01 GB → Plan is exhausted. Offer a top-up
- If data remains → Network/device issue, continue to next step

### Step 5: Correct coverage area
Verify the customer is in a country covered by their plan:
- A `US_10GB_30D` plan does NOT work in Canada or Mexico
- A `Europe_STANDARD` plan may not cover all European countries
- Check the coverage section for the specific plan type

### Step 6: Toggle airplane mode
This forces the phone to disconnect and re-register on the network:
1. Turn **Airplane Mode ON**
2. Wait **10 seconds**
3. Turn **Airplane Mode OFF**
4. Wait 1-2 minutes for the phone to reconnect

### Step 7: Restart the phone
A full restart clears network caches and forces a fresh connection:
1. Power off the phone completely
2. Wait 30 seconds
3. Power on
4. Wait 2-3 minutes for full network registration

### Step 8: Manual network selection
If the phone isn't connecting to the right network automatically:
- **iPhone:** Settings → Cellular → [TravelWifi plan] → Network Selection → turn OFF Automatic → manually select a network from the list
- **Samsung:** Settings → Connections → Mobile networks → Network operators → Search networks → select one
- **Pixel:** Settings → Network & internet → [SIM] → Advanced → Automatically select network → OFF → choose from list
- After connecting, turn Automatic back ON

### Step 9: Check APN settings
Usually auto-configured, but if needed:
- **iPhone:** Settings → Cellular → [TravelWifi plan] → Cellular Data Network → APN
- **Samsung:** Settings → Connections → Mobile networks → Access Point Names
- The correct APN depends on the TelliSIM network partner (varies by country)
- If APN is blank or wrong, try common defaults: `internet`, `data`, or contact support for the country-specific APN

# Troubleshooting — other common issues

## Slow data speeds
- eSIM plans do **NOT** have FUP or throttling — if speed is slow, it's a network issue, not a plan limitation
- Check if the customer is in a congested area (airports, stadiums, city centers during rush hour)
- Check remaining data — if nearly depleted, the network may deprioritize
- Toggle airplane mode to force reconnection to a potentially better tower
- Try manual network selection to pick a different carrier
- 3G fallback may occur in areas without 4G/LTE coverage — this is normal in some countries and rural areas
- Speed test: ask customer to try fast.com or speedtest.net to measure actual throughput

## Plan shows "expired" but customer just arrived
- The plan may have been activated unintentionally during a **layover** (phone auto-connected to a roaming network at a transit airport)
- Check `activation_at` in TelliSIM — if it activated days before the expected travel date, this likely happened
- Resolution: issue a replacement plan if the activation was clearly unintentional (e.g., 2-hour layover consumed a 7-day plan)
- Prevention: advise customers to keep the eSIM **disabled** until they arrive at their final destination

## Customer can't find the eSIM on their phone
- **iPhone:** Settings → Cellular → look for the plan label (may say "Travel", "Secondary", or the carrier name)
- **Samsung:** Settings → Connections → SIM manager → look for the eSIM entry
- **Pixel/Android:** Settings → Network & internet → SIMs
- If the eSIM doesn't appear at all, it may not have installed correctly — check if the QR code was fully scanned
- On iPhone, also check: Settings → General → About → look for a second carrier listing

## "No Service" shown on phone
- This usually means the eSIM is not connecting to any network
- Verify the customer is in a coverage area for their plan
- Check that data roaming is ON
- Toggle airplane mode
- If persistent, the eSIM profile may be corrupted — consider removing and requesting a new QR code

## Phone says "SOS only" or "Emergency calls only"
- The eSIM is registered on a network but data service is not authorized
- Usually means the plan hasn't activated yet or has expired
- Check TelliSIM lifecycle status
- If ACTIVE with data remaining, try airplane mode toggle or manual network selection

## Data usage seems too high
- Cloud backup services (iCloud, Google Photos, OneDrive) can consume GB of data without the customer realizing
- Automatic app updates (App Store / Play Store) can also drain data
- Video streaming in HD/4K uses 2-3 GB per hour
- Advise customer to check: Settings → Cellular → scroll down to see per-app data usage (iPhone)
- Advise customer to check: Settings → Network → Data usage (Android)

# Frequently asked questions

## Can I use eSIM for calls and texts?
No. TravelWifi eSIM plans are **data-only**. For calls and texts, customers should use apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, FaceTime, or Skype over the data connection. Their regular phone number and primary SIM remain active for receiving calls/texts (the primary carrier may charge international roaming fees for incoming calls — that's between the customer and their home carrier).

## Can I share my eSIM data with other devices?
Yes, using the phone's **Personal Hotspot** (tethering) feature. The phone acts as a WiFi hotspot and other devices (laptop, tablet, partner's phone) connect through it. Note that hotspot usage consumes data faster — especially with multiple devices connected.
- **iPhone:** Settings → Personal Hotspot → Allow Others to Join → ON
- **Samsung:** Settings → Connections → Mobile Hotspot and Tethering → Mobile Hotspot → ON
- **Pixel:** Settings → Network & internet → Hotspot & tethering → Wi-Fi hotspot → ON

## Can I use the eSIM in multiple countries?
- **Local plans:** No — a local plan (e.g., `US_10GB_30D`) only works in that specific country
- **Regional plans:** Yes — a regional plan works in all countries within that tier's coverage
- **Global plans:** Yes — a global plan works in all covered countries worldwide
- Switching between countries within a regional/global plan is automatic — no action needed from the customer

## What happens when my plan expires?
The eSIM stops providing data. The eSIM profile remains on the phone but is inactive. The customer can:
1. Purchase a **top-up** to add more data to the same eSIM
2. Purchase a **new plan** for a different destination
3. Remove the eSIM profile from their phone (optional — inactive eSIMs don't cause issues)

## Can I get a refund if I didn't use all my data?
Refund policies vary. Check the customer's order status and usage:
- If the plan was **never activated** (PENDING state, 0 bytes used) — typically eligible for a full refund
- If the plan was **partially used** — review on a case-by-case basis
- If the plan was **mostly consumed then cancelled** — potential friendly fraud. Check the >80% consumption flag before processing

## How many eSIMs can I have on my phone?
Most modern phones support 5-10 eSIM profiles simultaneously, though only one or two can be active at a time. Customers can switch between active eSIMs in their phone settings.
- **iPhone 14-16 (US):** Up to 8 eSIMs (no physical SIM tray)
- **iPhone XS-13:** 1 physical SIM + 1 active eSIM (can store multiple, toggle between them)
- **Samsung/Pixel:** Typically 1 physical SIM + 1 active eSIM
- Stored but inactive eSIMs do not use data or battery

## Does the eSIM work on tablets and smartwatches?
- **Tablets** with cellular capability (e.g., iPad with cellular, Samsung Tab with LTE) — Yes, if the tablet supports eSIM
- **Smartwatches** — Generally no. Most smartwatch eSIMs are carrier-locked to the watch's paired phone plan and don't support third-party travel eSIMs

## Is my data secure on an eSIM?
Yes. eSIM data connections are encrypted the same way as regular SIM connections. The eSIM profile itself is securely stored in the phone's embedded chip (eUICC) and cannot be extracted, cloned, or transferred to another device.

## Can I keep my phone number while using TravelWifi eSIM?
Yes. The TravelWifi eSIM only handles **data**. Your regular phone number stays on your primary SIM/eSIM. You'll continue to receive calls and texts on your normal number (your home carrier may charge roaming fees for those — that's separate from TravelWifi).

## What if I lose my phone?
- The eSIM profile is tied to the phone's hardware — it cannot be recovered on a new phone
- The plan data will continue counting down until the validity expires
- Contact support for a potential credit or replacement plan on the new device
- The lost phone's eSIM profile cannot be used by anyone else without unlocking the phone
